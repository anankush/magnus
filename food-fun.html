BBIT Food Carnival, Clubs & Fun Zones

Beyond competitive tournaments, BBIT Magnus transforms the campus into a carnival of flavors, student innovation stalls, interactive fun games, and cultural club displays!

Zone B: The BBIT Grand Food Court & Canteen Lawns

Over 25+ food stalls serving fresh, delicious, and pocket-friendly Kolkata delicacies from 09:30 AM to 10:00 PM.

Kolkata Street Treats

Famous Kolkata Nizam-style egg-chicken rolls, crispy phuchka, ghugni, and hot Darjeeling momos.

Price: ₹30 – ₹120

Royal Biryani & Mughlai

Fragrant Kolkata mutton & chicken biryani with aloo, chicken chaap, parathas, and butter chicken rice bowls.

Price: ₹120 – ₹220

Dessert Bar & Chillers

Belgian waffle sticks, sizzling brownies with ice cream, matka kulfi, cold coffee, and virgin mojitos.

Price: ₹50 – ₹150

Active BBIT Student Societies

Meet the student bodies driving creativity and technological prowess at Budge Budge Institute of Technology:

  • GDG on Campus BBIT (Google Developer Groups): Live coding helpdesks, web3 demos, and tech quizzes.
  • BBIT Robotics Club: Autonomous maze solvers, drone flight simulator, and combat bot exhibitions.
  • Natya & Drama Troupe: High-impact Nukkad Natak (street drama) addressing contemporary social issues.
  • BBIT Lens & Shutter: Campus photography contests, photo-booths with fun props, and portrait sessions.
  • Rotaract & Eco Club: Clean campus drive, paperless digital passes, and eco-friendly fest decor.

Campus Fun & Carnival Games

Chill out between fixtures and win exciting BBIT Magnus merchandise and restaurant vouchers:

  1. 20-Acre Campus Treasure Hunt: Decipher cryptic riddles hidden across BBIT landmarks to win cash prizes.
  2. Table Tennis & Chess Arena: Casual drop-in games organized at the Dining Block and Student Lounge.
  3. Carnival Skill Booths: Balloon darts, ring toss, bucket-ball, and hammer smash for giant plushies.
  4. Open Acoustic Corner: Jam with guitarists, beatboxers, and vocalists at the Central Canteen lawn!

Want to Book a Food Stall or Club Kiosk?

Student commercial stalls and club booths can be reserved directly with the BBIT Food Committee.

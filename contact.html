How to Reach BBIT Campus

  • By Local Train (Sealdah South): Board Budge Budge local from Sealdah / Majerhat to Budge Budge Station (BBT). Take an auto or Toto directly to BBIT Campus Gate (10 mins).
  • By Bus: Route 77A from Esplanade / Taratala / Behala directly to Budge Budge. Auto services are available 24x7 from Taratala and Thakurpukur.
  • By College Special Transit: 15+ special fest bus shuttles will run between Howrah Station, Ultadanga, Ruby Hospital, and BBIT Campus during all 4 fest days.
  • Campus Parking: Secure on-campus 2-wheeler and 4-wheeler parking available inside the 20-acre complex.

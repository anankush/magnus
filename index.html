About BBIT & Magnus

Organized under the patronage of the Jagannath Gupta Family Trust, Budge Budge Institute of Technology (BBIT) is accredited by NAAC/NBA and affiliated with MAKAUT (WBUT). Its annual flagship festival Magnus spans our 20-acre lush green campus in Nischintapur, Budge Budge, Kolkata.

Things You Can Enjoy at Magnus

Four power-packed wings of non-stop action across our 20-acre campus:

  • Star-Studded Celebrity Concerts: Past headliners include Salim-Sulaiman, Vishal-Shekhar, Ayushmann Khurrana, and The Anupam Roy Band.
  • Renaissance (Cultural Arena): Battle of the Bands, Eastern & Western Group Dance, and Couture Fashion Runway.
  • Idrott (Inter-College Sports): 11-a-side Cricket on BBIT Main Ground, Football, Basketball, Kabaddi, and Badminton.
  • Armageddon (Esports League): High-speed LAN tournaments for BGMI, Free Fire MAX, Call of Duty Mobile, and console FIFA.
  • INNOV8 & TechSprint: 36-hour Hackathon, Robowar battles, and AI workshops in partnership with Google Developer Groups (GDG) on Campus BBIT.
  • Grand Food Court: 25+ famous Kolkata street food counters, royal biryani stalls, mocktails, and bakery pop-ups.

How to Register for Events

Follow these five simple steps to secure your college team's entry into the official tournament fixtures:

  1. Browse Category Rules: Visit the Events Schedule to check age criteria, squad sizes, venues, and registration fees.
  2. Obtain NOC / College Authorization: Keep scanned copies of participant Student IDs and College Bonafide/NOC letters ready for inter-college events.
  3. Submit Online Application: Fill the verified form on our Registration Portal with captain and squad details.
  4. Confirm Slot & Payment: Complete online UPI payment or opt for cash verification at the BBIT Fest Helpdesk (SAC Block).
  5. Collect Entry QR Pass: Download your personalized QR access badge for security checkpoint entry at BBIT Gate No. 1.
